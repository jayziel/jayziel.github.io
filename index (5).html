How I Work

From revenue leak to a tested, documented working system. I start with the business problem, then design a practical system that can be tested, maintained, explained, and handed off.

01 DISCOVER

Map the customer journey

Identify where leads enter, where they drop off, and where manual follow-up creates gaps.

02 DESIGN & BUILD

Turn the solution into a system

Structure the CRM, pipeline logic, triggers, conditions, and follow-up around the workflow.

03 TEST & REFINE

Test real-world scenarios

Check timing, conditions, edge cases, duplicate actions, and handoffs before launch.

04 DOCUMENT & HAND OFF

Make it clear and maintainable

Document the logic, workflows, and key settings so the system can be understood and managed.
